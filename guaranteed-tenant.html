Why isn't this listed publicly on FlatKhoj?

Because we're personally guaranteeing an outcome on this listing, it's handled by a dedicated, smaller set of broker partners who've committed to this program — not shown on the open marketplace. This keeps focus on your property and protects the partner broker actually doing the work, rather than having the listing picked up by unrelated parties along the way.

One condition: while your listing is active in this program, FlatKhoj is your only channel for finding a tenant — please pause other brokers or listings for this property during this period. Full terms will be shared before you sign up.
