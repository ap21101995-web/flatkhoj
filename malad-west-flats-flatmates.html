Flats, Flatmates & PGs in Malad West, Mumbai

Malad West has grown rapidly around the Mindspace and Infinity Mall IT corridor, drawing a steady flow of professionals working in the BPO, IT, and media firms clustered there. It sits directly on the Western Line with quick access to both Goregaon and Kandivali, and offers a noticeably wider range of housing stock — from older buildings near the station to newer towers along Link Road.

Rents remain relatively moderate for a Western Line suburb — a single-occupancy room in a shared flat typically runs ₹13,000–₹20,000, and a 1BHK ₹20,000–₹35,000, with buildings closer to Mindspace and Infinity Mall commanding a premium over the station-side localities.
