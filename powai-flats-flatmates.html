Flats, Flatmates & PGs in Powai, Mumbai

Powai is Mumbai's lake-side tech and business hub — home to IIT Bombay, a dense cluster of IT parks, and the planned Hiranandani Gardens township. It draws a heavy concentration of working professionals and students, and is one of the few Mumbai suburbs built around wide roads and organised housing rather than older organic development.

Rents reflect its planned, premium character — a single-occupancy room in a shared flat typically runs ₹18,000–₹28,000, and a 1BHK ₹30,000–₹55,000, with Hiranandani Gardens at the top end and the areas closer to Chandivali and Powai Lake more moderate.
