Common questions
Do I need coins to post a listing?
No — posting is always free. Coins are only needed to upgrade your daily/monthly posting limit.
How long does the upgrade last?
Exactly 30 days from the day you upgrade. After 30 days your plan auto-reverts to Free (1 post/day, 3 posts/month). You will need to pay coins again to renew. There is no auto-renewal — your coins are only spent when you choose to upgrade.
What happens to my coin balance after upgrading?
The coins are deducted from your balance immediately. Make sure you have enough coins before upgrading.
Where do I buy coins?
Go to Buy Coins — packs start at just ₹49 for 10 coins.
