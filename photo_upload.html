📷 Photo tips
Take photos in good lighting — open curtains and switch on lights
Show the bedroom, bathroom, kitchen and living area if possible
Landscape (horizontal) photos look better on the listing
Avoid blurry or very dark photos — they reduce trust
⚠️ Photo rules
Only real photos of the flat — no screenshots, flyers or posters
No phone numbers, names or watermarks in photos — these will be removed automatically
No explicit, inappropriate or offensive content
Violations may result in listing removal or account ban
