Flats, Flatmates & PGs in Ghatkopar West, Mumbai

Ghatkopar West benefits from a genuinely rare combination in Mumbai — it's where the Central Line suburban rail meets the Metro 1 (Andheri-Ghatkopar) line, making it one of the easiest suburbs to reach both South Mumbai and the western business districts from. This connectivity has driven steady redevelopment, with older chawls and low-rise buildings increasingly giving way to taller residential towers.

Rents reflect this connectivity premium — a single-occupancy room in a shared flat typically runs ₹14,000–₹20,000, and a 1BHK ₹22,000–₹38,000, with buildings closer to the Metro/station interchange commanding a premium over the interior lanes.
