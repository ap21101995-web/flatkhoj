Flats, Flatmates & PGs in Vile Parle, Mumbai

Vile Parle sits right between Andheri and Santacruz, and is best known for its strong presence of colleges (NMIMS, Narsee Monjee) and proximity to the domestic airport terminal. This makes it a steady market for both student flatmate searches and working professionals who want quick airport access without paying Bandra or Juhu-level rents.

Rents here run roughly ₹16,000–₹24,000 for a single-occupancy room in a shared flat, and ₹28,000–₹48,000 for a 1BHK, with Vile Parle West generally pricier than the East side due to better connectivity to the station and Juhu.
